I'm familiar with the idea of "social bookmarking", a practice where one could use a service like
[del.icio.us][1] to capture things and links that they've found interesting. I used to have
something similar on my own website prior my "reset" earlier in July, which was helpful. I could
look up a particular link, if I couldn't recall the title. I'm also one of those folks who'd use
their browser's bookmarking function to keep track of things like that &mdash; relying on history
alone wasn't enough as I tend to clear it.

For this site, the process is a lot more manual. My former site was capable of syndicating whatever
I posted here to places like BlueSky and Mastodon after I published it. In this case, I'll have to
write it on my computer, deploy it (make it live on the Internet) and then post manually to my
BlueSky and Mastodon accounts respectively. This is going to be a bit of a forcing function to help
me determine what I want this process to look like. I'm in a good groove of development with Livtet
which is freeing up a bit of thinking space to reconsider what I'm doing with this site. I still have
[the same constraints I outlined][2] that I don't plan on changing, to keep my costs down.

The first link I've written was about [the right to strike from DISSENT magazine][3]! Let's see what else
I can muster up; I have a lot in the backlog.
