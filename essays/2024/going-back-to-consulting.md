Technically, I've been consulting since the start of September 2024. But I'm writing this after I've done
the paperwork and registered as a local company. The hope is to work with and help out organizations
&mdash; big or small &mdash; to build out online and offline technical solutions for their approaches.
There's a bit of a revival of what I've done with [black.af][3][^1] but a branching out. It's to also
serve as an umbrella for other things like publishing paid games I'm working on[^2], holding all of the
IP[^2] for things I work on like my short stories, novellas and the like. I'm not looking for "make
it big" kind of money; just enough to validate and sustain the ideas explored here. I'm hoping to
keep it transparent like organizations like [Hello Code][1] or [Buffer][2] because capitalism
_thrives_ in obscurity. There's a lot that I'd wish to do but here's to hoping that this allows me
to fund those things in such a way that they become easier to do.
