I'm (forever) working on what I want this website to be like. I've stopped imagining it as some bastion of a
"quantified digital self", as difficult as that's showing to be since blogs and the Web are designed
to [be uniform in its chronological progression][1]. Additionally, I find that to be the de-facto
orientation and design of the Web as outlined and commanded by [Google since 1999][2], anything
that's not meant to be commercially beneficial falls back to the obsession of chronological sorting.
It's not a golden rule &mdash; a means of breaking away from that is by allowing an alternate algorithm
create a different kind of river of content that you can then exchange for hours of your life in
idle motion. However, [this requires a bit of effort][3].
