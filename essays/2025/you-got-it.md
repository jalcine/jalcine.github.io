I'm writing this way later than I'd care to. I saw a post that reiterated that it was _solely_ the
action of voters of Trump that led to the formation of the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) and the
Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) that's fueled the current state of (extended) crisis for
those who have seen this harm be perpetuated for decades now (and an extension of it for even
longer). This chosen sports-like behavior, "it's their fault!", "they opted for it", allows the
State to run blameless, unchecked and unaccountable for its action.

This violence we see today is what those who are impacted by immigration on a personal level[^1] see
happen to them _irregardless_ of who is in charge because *this is the modus operandi* of American
domestic policy. **Dissent is not to be tolerated**, as described by President Barack Obama during
the Ferguson uprising just as President Trump declared implicitly after the "illegal"[^2] arrest of
a Palestinian student advocate. The level of anger is righteous and should be made into the opposing
party's line of defense. But this can't happen as long as that party remains the center-right one.

So, for those who are playing into the idea of sports-driven politics, please reserve that energy
for community action and aid. Talk to the people who need defense instead of throwing digital
tomatoes at people who won't _ever_ see or care about what you post into a digital void.

## An Addendum
I really need people to stop scrolling and read a bit more. Namely two books, [It Did Happen
Here][1] and [The Black Antifascist Tradition][2]; because what we're seeing is a _very_ American
tradition and it's _integral_ to its operation. Neither major party will make efforts to dismantle
the systems that they profit from in social, political and economic class.

    imagine the labor needed to be in a farm or similar climate on a regular basis. We're removed
    from it, by design.
[^2]: Legality of an action is something that's left to be contested in a court; since that's the
    domain of law. For those who don't play into the "law and order" logics of America, we know that

    means of representation to defend yourself.
