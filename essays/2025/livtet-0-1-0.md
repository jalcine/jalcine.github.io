Livtet, on its head, seems like a relatively straightforward application. Its
goal is to keep a list of resources by a set of URL references with associated
metadata. That's the _underlying_ part of it. Plugins are provided to give more
decoration to the data and expanded logic of the app. On its head, it's a note
taking application!
