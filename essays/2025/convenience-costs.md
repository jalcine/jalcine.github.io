Wrestling with the luxury of being a citizen of the United States[^1] is this notion of "escape".
It's the idea that one can, as simply as they wrestle for a concert ticket, a new electronic device
or fashion item, select a new country to become a resident of. It dances with the idea of an
eventual return: folks rarely travel beyond the concept of just _travelling_ there. Americans lack
an understanding outside of the temporary actions of tourism that they'd invoke with visa stamp
collection in a passport defended by a military quite notably violent in the history of the modern
world. The pleasure of "constantly on" Internet, paved roads, always available entertainment and
spectacle &mdash; this is mirrored in other countries who align with the Western power bloc. Parts
of the Western powers are beginning to see fissures in this, like France and its slip of neocolonial
control over countries in the Sahel Belt.
