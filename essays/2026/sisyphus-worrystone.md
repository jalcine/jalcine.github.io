I've had this version of my static site using [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/)
for some time. It really does the job and I've enjoyed my use of it. However,
I'm slightly slamming against limits that I don't want to deal with it. I've
begun working on yet another CMS attempt, codenamed "ticaye" (_little house_ in
Haitian Kreyol), but I had to put that off to the side because of my need to
work on more immediate projects - livtet and divine being the most recent. I'm
figuring that also by blogging, I'll get a bit of focus on what it is I want
and how I can shape my experience towards that. There's quite a bit I want my
site to do and it all floats around "sovereignty":

- writing my blog posts as normal
- making my [weeknotes](/weeknotes), [linklog](/links) and other short(er) form posts
- use my site as my canonical place to post into the open social Web
- allow for deep, Wordpress-level theming and content editing
- keep stats on the things I use for easier reference in conversation and research

Some of this is easy to do. I made a page to track the tech and services I have
and use at [/uses](/uses) &mdash; open to feedback. I was debating adding
images but didn't think it was that useful. This link does help me glance over
stats as I'm actively looking to upgrade my home PC and going to my website is
a bit easier than SSH'ing into my home computing setup to figure this out. It
also helps me talk about my setup in general &mdash; I like talking about my
stuff!

But I think I'm just going to do something with this site until I get the new one going.
In a way, I'm trying to prevent this site [from being too quiet][1]. This
includes looking at my literal home lab seriously and writing more about it &mdash;
[like Andrew did][2]. It's kind of silly that I've had this hodgepodge setup
of a system for the last 6 years &mdash; might as well make it more useful.

## Operation `ticaye`

If you're someone who's on <https://lobste.rs> or
<https://news.ycombinator.com>, you'll see (although less nowadays) about the
perpetual site rewrite[^1]. The idea/hope is that I'll be concretely doing the
following:

- moving from <https://blacksky.app> as my [PDS][3] to my own home one
- having my site also lean into the [ActivityPub API][4] and [other IndieWeb specs][5]
- get more in-tune with the interface I want for publishing on my site

This will be the side-project that I'll churn on over the next year on weekends
when time permits. I _want this_ because I _like_ it. I'm in the same thinking
that [Andre](https://staltz.com) has in this post about some folks wanting to
[run some things at home][6] &mdash; some folks want to do their own gel nails,
touch up their cars, cook their own meals - and others don't mind making things
that they use that other people engage with (or at least perceive). If all else
fails, I'll just come back to <https://www.11ty.dev> and call it defeat.
