I don't have any cable (or neo-cable) subscriptions anymore. I cancelled my Spotify ones as well.
Being on their free plan is annoying enough that I'm tempted to delete my account. I do want to pay
for the media I'm into but I don't want to for the _slice_ of time in which I use it &mdash; I want
to actually _have_ the media. You know, like how folks would at least own DVDs in the 2000s[^1].
Ironically, in cancelling more of these subsctiptions, I had little reason to pull out my television
&mdash; I only recently did to be ready to drop into a game of Mario Kart with a friend. But even
that act feels too arresting &mdash; that of sitting in front of a TV. I just don't _care_ for it
anymore.
