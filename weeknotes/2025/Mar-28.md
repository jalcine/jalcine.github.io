I'm publishing this from [Central Library][1] in Brooklyn. After some time in San
Francisco, I've landed here and have been enjoying the brisk but sunny nature
of my hometown. I routinely feel sad about the speed of gentrification and
tourist-centric warping of my neighborhoods of my youth but this is to be
expected by a city that serves the class of folks making north of a quarter
million dollars a year[^1]. That aside, I really enjoy using the library as a
co-working space. It's refreshing to take some time writing and working;
peeking at a book on a shelf then taking a stroll by Prospect Park.

I missed last week's note mainly because of my time in San Francisco. I was
focused on being _aware_ which led to me doing things like watching [a ballet
performance of Frankenstein][2]. It took some creative liberties from the
source material from what I've been told but it didn't fail to fulfill its
goal. I spent a lot of time reading &mdash; print and digital works but have
been experiencing quite a bit of writers' block. I'm hoping to power through it
by reading some more.

I wrote about [what I'm doing with Livtet][3] in my downtime. I'm working on
another post about the (aggressive) use of SQL. It's been a while since I've
been able to interface with it so tightly. The main reason is to allow for
folks using the database _outside_ of Livtet to work with more guardrails to
prevent corruption. It's given me more appreciation for it as a portable
database format and made me curious about what else I can push into the
database itself.
