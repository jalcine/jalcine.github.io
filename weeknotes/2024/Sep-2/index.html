Reading

Surviving the Future

This book is written by people of all backgrounds and it's become more and more clear as it goes on. You might have seen a lot of my quotations of it on the Fediverse[1] — the essayists are not holding back. It's empowering to hear of actions folks are taking against establishment in the United States and Canada — the way media operates, you'd assume that liberation is something that pops up in February or June as a corporate branding event. I'm eager to finish this and add it to a list of books I want to write a longer review about.

I'm in need to catch up with books like The Trinity of Fundamentals and return to None but the Righteous; I'm happy to have a less "intense" reading journey. Very excited to add some fiction into the rotation.
