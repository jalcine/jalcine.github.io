# Reading
## [Surviving the Future][book:1]
This book is written by people of all backgrounds and it's become more and more clear as it goes on.
You might have seen a lot of my quotations of it on the Fediverse[^1] &mdash; the essayists are not
holding back. It's empowering to hear of actions folks are taking against establishment in the
United States and Canada &mdash; the way media operates, you'd assume that liberation is something
that pops up in February or June as a corporate branding event. I'm eager to finish this and add it
to a list of books I want to write a longer review about.

I'm in need to catch up with books like [The Trinity of Fundamentals][book:2] and return to [None
but the Righteous][book:3]; I'm happy to have a less "intense" reading journey. Very excited to add
some fiction into the rotation.

I've read and responded to some links I've seen around the Web:

* Anil Dash's [note on the mentality of billionaires][link:1] and [thoughts on how money moves for the wealthy][link:2]
* The Baffler with [thoughts on Kara Swisher as a tech journalist][link:3]

You can check out the rest of this at my [linklog][link]!
