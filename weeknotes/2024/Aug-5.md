# Reading

## 📕 [The Struggle Within][6]

    <img 
        src="/images/weeknotes/2024-08-05-struggle-within-page.jpg" 
        alt="The book 'The Struggle Within' leaning against two plant pots"

This book took me three days to read &mdash; I have so _many_ notes. There's movements I have no idea about like AIM or
the Puetro Rican liberation fronts. To see how much influence the Black Panthers had in these groups, as well as groups
like Weather Undeground makes the nature of COINTELPRO even more insidious &mdash; it being a system that works
to destroy and criminalize dissent. With Obama's work to make dissent basically unfavorable even amongst Black people
with his actions with Ferguson and the PATRIOT act, there's a big need for a cultural revolution that we can only begin
to fight for. I really can't recommend this book enough; dream hampton's afterword convinced me to reread [Assata
Shakur's autobiography][10].

## 📖 [Prisons Make Us Safer][1]
I picked this up immediately after _The Struggle Within_; another of the author's, Victoria Law, books is listed as
recommended reading. The books opens with a sobering statement: that the use of terms of like "inmate", "prisoner" and
the like are _all_ terms meant to dehumanize people. This is something I'm going to have to burn into my psyche as I
find myself thinking about this a lot; how language contributes to dehumanization, especially when it's handled by the
State.

## [The Tech Resume](https://thetechresume.com/)
Since I can't lose anything by reading a book on how to improve my resume (I also got it [for free][16]), I've begun
reading this book after breaking fast. I have to admit, my mobility in tech has been both through my skill and social
relations; so a resume wasn't as necessary for me (at least for screening). [I wrote about what I learned][19].

## 🔗 Around the Web
I'm going to try something a bit new! Here's some things I've read that were informative:

* [Labor Notes: New 'Battery Belt' Opens Organizing Front in the South][7]: A great and speedy article from the team
  that highlights the critical need for workers in the South to take this move to a new landscape to seize more worker
  power. Green capitalism is a _beast_ that washes liberals' feigned concerns with the climate crisis into something
  that we can allegedly fix by _paying the people who've contributed to it more money_.
* [Truthout: Bush's Infrastructure Bill Vote Criticized b Opponent and Amplified by AIPAC][9]: As misogynoir is a core
  component of American life and governing politics, it's not shocking to see this. What raised my eye was that she was
  working to bring more to the table; in a more honest representation of what a Green New Deal would have encompass
  (protections for people).
* [Futurism: Bumble and Hinge Let Creeps See Your Exact Location][8]: Another example of tech companies making tools
  that people at risk _first_ and needing the community to remind them that safety's important.
* [The Haitian Times: The other side of Cap-Haïtien's clean market: A pile of trash from it][14]: Another day, another
  example of the over-indexing for the wealthy communities in Haiti contributing to inequality; this time in sanitation.
  It's escalated to a point where Japan, on behalf of the United Nations, is willing to spend USD $2 million dollars on
  it. Note that nearly $35 million has been allegedly spent already &mdash; with nothing to show.
* [The Guardian: Antarctic temperatures rise 10C above average in near record heatwave][17]: Well. This feels like the
  new normal. The predator class is trying to play chicken with the planet and it's going to be at the cost of everyone
  else.
* [The Washington Post: How the Montgomery Riverfront brawl sparked an unofficial holiday][18]: What a time to be alive, ha.
