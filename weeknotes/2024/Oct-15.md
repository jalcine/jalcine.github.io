# Books
I finished [Black Software][book:1]; a read that begins with a journey of some folks who worked to
bring in Black faces into the burgeoning tech industry. It closes with a focus on how the American
government pioneered its War on Crime by using its historically racist approach to policing to
mechanize technology against the American public &mdash; specifically against non-white people. It
focuses on Black people but I can see these technologies being used against poor people of _any_
creed &mdash; to be poor is to be effectively Black by the edicts of American policy.

I'm currently finishing up [Their Eyes Were Watching God][book:2], a timely read that's a mix of an
adventure book and a series of romance happening right here in Florida. I'm extremely tempted to go
visit some of these places mentioned in the book like Eatonville and the Everglades. Hurtson has a
way with words that transport you right into the moment.

# New Job
Today's my first day working at [Coforma][] &mdash; a government IT company focused on providing
services for the American public. This continues my career focus on working in the public sector and
public-sector-adjacent places. I'm eager to talk more about what kind of work I'll be doing in the
coming months.

I have to thank _everyone_ who've helped me get to this point. I have a bigger plan to demonstrate
my appreciation that requires a bit of time to pass first but I'm really thankful and excited to see
where things will go from here.
