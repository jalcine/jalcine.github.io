## Links

I've been reading a lot of what other people have been writing; here's a list:

- [atproto-fact-labeler (beta) is routing existing fact-checks onto
  Bluesky][link:1]: This is _awesome_ and I'm glad that something like this is
  being built in the open. I _hate_ seeing bad posts that are nonfactual and
  provably false but being able to have something immediately highlight this is
  empowering.
- [Launching a Cloud Atlas][link:2]: Erin Kissane's notorious for being a great
  wordsmith about the open social Web (or the social Internet as she posits as a
  better moniker). I wrote more [about her piece][reply:1] and recommend it highly.
- [ATPROTO isn’t just for Bluesky weiners][link:3]: Dan Abramov has been doing
  a lot of interviews recently working to promote and legitimize ATProto as The
  New Social protocol for the Internet. This is validating my own work with
  [Olamaelcu][2]!
- [The End of Reading is Here][link:4]: Well. The title says it all but [I had
  more thoughts][reply:2] on this topic. It's hard reading this while working on
  an app focused on literate folks but never say never!
  [I felt worth expanding on][reply:3].
- [A Social Filesystem][link:7]: Now this was one that affirmed some stuff I
  knew. Dan worked at Bluesky and Facebook and has the ears of the tech
  industry's darlings. He supposes the idea of applying the personal computing
  concept of user-owned files to social media by creating a "social filesystem"
  where users maintain repositories containing all their data across different
  apps as structured records, rather than letting individual platforms store
  and control user content. This system, implemented through the AT Protocol,
  enables users to switch apps and hosting providers without losing their data,
  and allows multiple apps to work with the same data simultaneously, much like
  different programs can read the same file format. Safe to say that I'm
  _super_ invested.
- [Where It's at://][link:8]: This is a long blog post explaining how `at://`
  URLs work using Rudy's post as an example, lol.
- [AI as a Tool for the Mental Load][link:9]: I'm on the same page here. And
  frankly, as someone who's routinely overwhelmed, having a bit of a beep boop
  in the thinking flow has been helping.

Lots of posts about [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com/) mainly because I'm
building for and around it!
