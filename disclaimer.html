Disclaimers

I don't have a "set critiera" of things that I write about on my site or on my social media accounts. However, they do tend to have some levels of commonality that you'll find falls into one of the following zones below, in order of the chance of them being brought up. It goes without saying but nothing that I post, unless specified otherwise, is on behalf of my employers, past or present; I speak for me and myself alone.

  • Politics: While having a lot of critique of the Western hemisphere, my focus isn't constrained to a particular region. Due to me living with the United States of America and being of Haitian descent, you can almost assume it's in relation to these places.
  • Technology: Having been a technologist for some time now, I have a lot of thoughts on the field, the culture and the future of it.
  • Literature: The side effect of reading a lot is that you'll potentially have equally as much to say about what you've read. I keep track of what I read, keep notes and reviews of the such for future reflection.

It should go without saying that I'm not bound to any of the above and because I'm a being of free will; I'll deviate from this list.
