I've been paying attention to changes in this company's attempt to create _open
e-paper technology_. I'm quite a fan of the concept; it makes technology less
encroaching on one's attention and helps with reading in low light and low
power situations. In a world where everything is racing to (and passing!) 4K
imagery, it's great to see folks focused on keeping our eyesight and focus
intact.

Unfortunately, I don't have an immediate development use-case to purchase such
a device. I do hope this finds its way into those who are making things for
folks!
