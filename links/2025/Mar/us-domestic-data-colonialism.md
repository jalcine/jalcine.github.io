The concept of colonialism, the expanding of control of a people, their environment and culture, is
foreign to the American populace; at least those who are "citizen-native"[^1]. This article is
co-authored by the same authors of [Data Grab][1]. It's a succient explainer of what many folks have
been chided as "extreme", "ridiculous" or "outlandish" for: the _core_ purpose of the companies of
the Silicon Valley collective is to absorb, quantify and monetize as much information as possible
about _everyone_ who touches their systems. It defeats the breadth and scope of privacy that one
could advocate for[^2]. I'm appreciative of this article. I'm also confident that the folks who need
to read this _won't_ or will allow their corporate alliances to prevent them from understanding that
they aren't a comrade or even a customer to these companies: _they're a cog_.
