The video's creator warns early that this is a "old man yelling at cloud" kind of video and I think
they do themselves a disservice with that. The ability to search for information _is_ a skill on
decline and that's a big "winning" atttribute of technology: to remove as much labor as possible
from humans, regardless of the task. Need a house cleaner? There's an app for that! Need to dig your
heels into generational racist neighborhood watch policing? There's _multiple apps_ for that!
Looking to determine which women to prosecute based on their ovulation cycles? There's a plethora of
apps for that. Surveillance aside, technology is an amplifer of the underlying behaviors we have. It
can also introduce new ones that supplant others that we've developed along the way &mdash; like
searching.

The video goes on to explain how despite this, YouTube still provides a way out for folks. I avoid
using the YouTube app directly and use [FreeTube][^1] to follow folks I'm wanting to stay up to date
with and to open any YouTube links as a whole. Most of the money made from YouTube isn't from ads
either; it's largely paid members and views (which are tied together; you're more likely to get the
former when you can juice the latter &mdash; an extremely equitable platform)[^2].
