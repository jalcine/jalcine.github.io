Over the last few days, I've been speed running (at 1.75x) a podcast by
[Evan][1] called <https://revolution.social/>, which is a bit of a build-up to
what his company is building; a "permission-less" social media network. I am a
bearish person by trait and do not move under the notion of "assuming positive
intent" &mdash; that has to be earned. That had me struggle with some of the
insight that Evan provided during his podcast due to what I'd label as a _very_
utopian, _"moun nan mond li a"_ (people only of his world), perspective on the
future of social media. Due to his career, access and social circles, he's been
able to host a genre of folks to talk and probe questions about their
perspectives on social media and how it's impacted them as well as a bit of a
biographical dump for each person. It's also preventing him from seeing the
locked-in levers of comfort that he sits in and how it still reinforces the
systems (and not create a system of dual power) on the systems he claims to be
against. To a degree, I wonder if the social ties (and access) is more
important to him than the actual principles he espouses &mdash; that's
problematic because that makes it easy to be sold.
