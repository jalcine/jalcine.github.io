I listened to this episode while doing some cleaning this morning. [Tracy's someone][1] I know of during
my time in California. I learned more about her from this interview than I anticipated[^1]. Her work
with [BlockParty][2] is something I've mentioned to friends and I'm glad to see that it survived the
mayhem wrought by Musk and has expanded beyond that platform. I do have to say, I'm slightly
disappointed in how little questioning (or the lack of depth) that I found with the interviewers had
with Tracy. She's a really sharp person and I would have loved to hear more about how she's managed
to settle on the practices that BlockParty promotes through its use.

You can jump about 19 minutes in to hear her segment[^2] and what she speaks on. I hope that such a
platform is something that grows to become less necessary as companies begin to give people more
levers for autonomy, a necessary component for privacy, and people begin to take their own privacy
more seriously.
