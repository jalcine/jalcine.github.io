Facebook is an advertising company. It makes a considerable amount of money from advertising[^1].
This is not to be shocking but a warning. The fact that someone has managed to flip it in the app is
indicative of whatever soft attempt that they had with "joining the Fediverse" could introduce them
scooping up the open social Web as part of a larger advertising network _beyond_ their walls. App
developers of the Fediverse _need_ to brace themselves for this.

The head of product [mentioned this intent][2]. If anything, we need to get more serious about
enabling _some_ means of "creator sustainability" in the Fediverse; being open isn't enough now. The
infection of surveillance capitalism into the open social Web has been here.
