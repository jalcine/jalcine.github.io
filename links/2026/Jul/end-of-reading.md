According to this piece, reading rates in America have collapsed - fewer than
half of adults read a book in 2022 (which - eh - I feel like this is hard to
assess), daily pleasure reading fell from 28% to 16% (which is both real and
wild; if reading is dead then folks are going to be just told what to think)
across (and this hurts me to repeat) _every age, gender, and education level_,
even as total words consumed explode via texts, posts, and feeds. Reading built
and freed the modern mind (logic, sustained attention, rational analysis), and
its erosion is reshaping cognition, politics (Trump as the first "postliterate
president," whose rhetorical style suits oral culture and yet challenges a
truly democratic one, even those with a farce of an existence as that of
America), and democratic institutions that assumed informed, literate citizens.
Generative AI now threatens writing itself - which is how people figure out
what they think - risking the loss of deep thinking and independent judgment,
making the literate era look like a brief anomaly between the oral and digital
ages. This has been hard for me to assess with even my own heavy use of
generative AI but damn does the prospect of folks just _not_ giving a fuck
anymore is scaring me.
