This will have spoilers for the TV show, _For All Mankind_.

I've been enjoying my binge watching of this show. My friend's wife suggested
it and I've been hooked since. There's points of the show that make my leftist
brain itch too much but it's part of the narrative of what makes America
_America_ - its jingoistic, racist but ever-curious, persistently curious and
overly ambitious goal to get ahead by any means necessary.

Now Season 3 is where things get _very_ interesting. Due to the work of the
public sector, the private sector is able to capitalize on the development of
space travel and end up competing with two national superpowers to make it to
Mars. At this point, the show enters full-on fantasy (one - folks are going to
Mars and we elected a woman President who's bad at hiding that she's gayer than
a chaise lounge). We also get to see the exceedingly annoying hubris of a man
be the guiding factor of NASA finally be matched by his inability to be a
decent role model for men leading to him being indirectly cucked by his best
friend's son.

![A scene from the Boondocks where a waiter says "wouldn't let that happen to me though." as he walks off](/images/not-to-me.gif)

The science of the show is _very_ cool including things not yet fully proven or
has failed in the past like [helium-3][2] and [underwater rocket launches][3].
I also learned about the deep-faking technology they used for [the Reagan and
Nixon era][1]. It's an interesting show because it's clearly science fiction
but also is toying on the edge of literal fiction. I will say though - this
show both reinforces the tropes of how the Soviet Union depicted themselves in
yesteryear and has me _way more curious_ on watching older Russian television
to see how they were _actually_ depicted. At times, it feels like there's some
directive by the State Department that all TV production studios are required
to produce a uniform depiction of what Russians look like. I feel like it was
one of the _cheapest_ parts of the show.

With all my misgivings, I think this show is great. I'm planning on finishing it!
